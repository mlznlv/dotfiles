# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive project community. Participation should make the project safer and easier to use for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting corrections.
- Share relevant context and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and contributor time.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberately introducing secrets, unsafe instructions, or malicious code.
- Sustained disruption of project discussions.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where a participant represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For confidential reports, use the repository's private vulnerability reporting channel when available and identify the report as a conduct concern. Otherwise, contact the maintainer through a private method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not publish sensitive details in an issue.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or block participants when necessary. Reports will be reviewed as privately and impartially as practical.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant and adapted for this project's current maintainer model.
